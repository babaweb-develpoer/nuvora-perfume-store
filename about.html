Our story

We opened Nuvora because buying good perfume in Pakistan meant guessing. Duty-free counters on the way home, market stalls with no guarantee, websites that wanted your card number before you had held the bottle.

So we went to the source

We work with four independent houses and buy from each of them directly. Every bottle reaches our shelves sealed, and leaves them the same way. Keeping the range small means we know every fragrance in it by heart, from the first spray to the last trace on a scarf the next day.

It also means we can be honest. If a perfume is too heavy for a Karachi summer, we will say so. If you want something warm for a December wedding, we will point you to one we have actually worn.

We pack every order ourselves and send it out cash on delivery, so the first time you pay is the moment the parcel is in your hands.

The person answering your message has worn it.

What we promise

Three things, on every order, without exceptions.

  • Sealed, and straight from the house

    Every bottle comes to us factory-sealed from the house that made it, and reaches you with that seal intact. Check it at the door before you pay. If a bottle we sold you is ever shown not to be genuine, we refund you in full.

  • Pay when it is in your hands

    Every order is cash on delivery. No card details, no advance transfer. We confirm the order with you first, and you pay the courier only when the parcel arrives.

  • Packed as if it were a gift

    Each bottle travels in its own box, wrapped in tissue and braced inside a rigid carton so it cannot shift. Gift wrap in our black box with a gold ribbon costs a small fee per order.

How an order travels

From the moment you press order to the knock at your door.

  1. 1

    You order

    Choose your fragrances and check out with your name, address and mobile number. Nothing is paid online.

  2. 2

    We confirm

    Within one working day we get in touch to confirm the bottles, the address and the total, so the courier finds you first time.

  3. 3

    We dispatch

    Your order is packed by hand and handed to our courier within 1 to 2 working days of confirmation. Follow it on Track your order.

  4. 4

    It arrives

    Once dispatched, delivery takes 2 to 5 working days after dispatch, depending on your city. Check the seal, then pay the courier in cash.

Delivery times by city, charges and returns are all on Shipping & returns.
