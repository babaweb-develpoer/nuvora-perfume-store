Shipping

We deliver to every city and town in Pakistan that our courier partner reaches. Orders are packed by hand and handed to the courier within 1 to 2 working days of being confirmed. Working days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.

How long delivery takes

Typical delivery times, in working days after dispatch
Where you are Includes Working days
Major cities Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi 2 to 3
Other cities Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Sialkot, Gujranwala, Hyderabad, Quetta and similar 3 to 4
Rest of Pakistan Smaller towns and rural addresses 3 to 5

Northern areas can take a day or two longer when roads close for weather. We will tell you on the confirmation call if we expect a delay.

What delivery costs

Delivery is a flat fee per order, wherever you are. It is free when your order comes to our free-delivery amount or more, counted before any discount code and gift wrap. Your bag always shows how far you are from free delivery.

Cash on delivery

Every order is cash on delivery. Nothing is charged online and we never ask for card details or an advance transfer.

The confirmation call

Within one working day of your order, we contact you on the number you gave at checkout to confirm the bottles, your address and the total. Your order is dispatched once you confirm. If we cannot reach you after two tries over two days, we cancel the order and let you know by text, so nothing is sent that you did not expect.

Paying the courier

When the parcel arrives, check that the box and the seal on each bottle are intact, then pay the courier the exact total in cash. Keep notes ready: couriers often carry very little change. The courier gives you a receipt, and Track your order shows the order as delivered.

If delivery fails

The courier calls before each attempt and makes up to two. If nobody is available, or the parcel is refused without a reason, it comes back to us and the order is cancelled. You pay nothing. To order again, simply place a new order; we may ask you to confirm it by phone first.

Returns & exchanges

Changed your mind? You can return or exchange any bottle within 7 days of delivery, provided it is unopened: cellophane and seal intact, in its original box with everything it came with. Opened bottles can only be returned if they arrived damaged or faulty, because we can no longer sell them as sealed.

How to return a bottle

  1. Tell us within 7 days. Message us through the contact page or on WhatsApp with your order number and which bottle you are returning.
  2. Pack it up. Put the sealed bottle back in its box and in the original parcel if you still have it.
  3. We collect it. Our courier picks it up from your address at no cost to you, usually within 3 working days.
  4. We check and refund. Once the bottle reaches us and the seal is confirmed intact, we refund or send your exchange.

Exchanges

You can swap an unopened bottle for a different fragrance or size. If the new bottle costs more, you pay the difference in cash when it is delivered; if it costs less, we refund the difference.

How refunds are paid

As you paid in cash, we refund by bank transfer or mobile wallet, whichever you prefer, within 5 working days of the bottle reaching us. Refunds cover the price of the bottle and any gift wrap. The delivery charge is refunded too when the mistake was ours.

Damaged in transit

We pack every bottle to survive a long road, but parcels are sometimes dropped. If the outer box is crushed or wet, or you can smell perfume through it, you may refuse it at the door and we will send a replacement at no cost.

If you find damage after opening, message us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the bottle and the box. We replace it free of charge, or refund you in full including delivery if you prefer, and there is no need to send the broken bottle back.

Gift wrapping

Tick gift wrap next to any bottle in your bag and it leaves us in our black gift box, bedded in ivory satin and tied by hand with a gold ribbon. Wrapping costs a small fee once per order, however many bottles you wrap.

Buying more than one gift? The code GIFT15 takes a percentage off orders of a set amount or more. Gift wrap is refunded along with the bottle if it comes back unopened.

Something else?

Most questions are answered in our FAQ. For anything about a specific order, contact us with your order number and we will take it from there.
